# Research sources

The source code is distributed under the repository MIT license. The corrected
alpha/sheaf construction is independently implemented from the mathematical
definitions cited in the manuscript; upstream PSL source is not vendored.

MDG benchmark structures and annotations are external research inputs. Their
origin, archive hashes, parsing decisions, and exclusions are documented in
`docs/research_provenance.md` and the generated dataset manifest. Raw inputs are
not redistributed. RCSB coordinates used for figures retain their structure
identifiers and retrieval provenance. New results and figures are generated by
the scripts; historical numerical snapshots are explicitly separated.
